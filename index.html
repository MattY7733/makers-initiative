Who we are

High schoolers, real engineering, real communities.

📍 California Academy of Mathematics and Science (CAMS)

We're a group of high schoolers from California Academy of Mathematics and Science who wanted to do more with what we were learning than turn it in for a grade. So we started building things for people who could actually use them.

If you're a teacher, nonprofit, or neighbor with a problem that a small engineered project could help solve — a tool, a device, a piece of assistive tech — we'd genuinely love to hear from you. That's the whole reason this club exists.

No experience is required to work with us, on either side: you don't need to know engineering to bring us an idea, and our members don't need prior experience to join and build it.

What we build

Real projects, on a two-week clock.

Every two weeks we scope, design, and ship one project for a small or underserved community, then start the next one. It's a sprint: short enough to stay focused, real enough that someone is actually waiting to use what we make.

Past builds have ranged from robotic arms to assistive devices — whatever the community we're paired with actually needs.

No experience required. Bring curiosity — we'll teach the tools.
  1. 1

    Find the need

    We partner with a local group or community and scope a real problem worth solving.

  2. 2

    Design & build it

    We sketch, prototype, and build the solution over the following two weeks.

  3. 3

    Deliver & repeat

    We hand it off to the people it was built for, then start scoping the next sprint.

Subteams

Four crews, one build.

Every sprint pulls on all four subteams. Pick the one closest to your interest — you'll pick up the rest as you go.

Programming

Programming

Writes the software side of the projects: firmware, apps, and dashboards.

CAD

CAD

Designs and models the build, turning concepts into something we can fabricate.

Electrical

Electrical

Wires and designs the electronics that power each build.

Business

Business

Budgets, sources parts, and manages the communities we partner with.
